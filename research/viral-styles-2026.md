## Six original reference styles

| ID | Best-fit niches | Hook mechanism | Evidence graphic | Emotional payoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `storyflow` | AI / technology / explainers | A question turns into a signal trail | Connected concept graph | Clear aha |
| `curiosity-lab` | everyday science / unusual facts | Unexpected result before the explanation | Visual experiment / before vs after | Discovery |
| `data-shock` | tech statistics / economics / comparison | Counterintuitive number with a meaningful unit | Animated scale comparison | Recalibration |
| `myth-breaker` | digital literacy / tech misconceptions | Common belief interrupted by verifiable proof | Myth → test → evidence | Relief / confidence |
| `micro-drama` | history / biographies / creator journeys | Conflict at a consequential moment | Character objective and obstacle | Resolution |
| `product-xray` | useful products / affiliate / gadgets | Visible problem and direct demonstration | Mechanism / comparison / tradeoff | Practical decision |

## Hook design (0–5 seconds)

**0–1 s:** show the contradiction, question, visible problem or result before any logo/intro.
**1–3 s:** provide a credible visual reason to stay (the unexpected evidence, not a vague promise).
**3–5 s:** establish the real stakes and start the explanation. Avoid lengthy channel greetings.
**Middle:** evidence must evolve visually, not be disguised as slides. Add a concrete new observation per beat.
**Ending:** satisfy the opening premise, then invite a specific useful action. No deceptive cliffhanger.

Suggested durations (test, don't assume): 15–25 seconds for one fact, 25–45 seconds for one explainable mechanism, 45–75 seconds for an evidence-heavy story. Match length to information density, not arbitrary cuts.

## Indonesian examples — ready for experimentation

1. `curiosity-lab` — "Kenapa es di gelas ini cepat mencair, tapi yang ini tidak?" Show two cups before the question.
2. `myth-breaker` — "Mode incognito benar-benar bikin kamu anonim?" Show browser icon and a transparent connection diagram; avoid false security claims.
3. `data-shock` — "Berapa lama sebenarnya 1 juta detik?" Reveal a time-scale comparison with exact calculation.
4. `micro-drama` — "Satu keputusan kecil ini mengubah sejarah komputer." Identify a verifiable event and show its consequences.
5. `product-xray` — "Benda murah ini mengatasi masalah apa?" Start with a real, demonstrable problem; disclose paid affiliation.
6. `storyflow` — "Ke mana sebenarnya pertanyaanmu pergi saat memakai AI?" Track a conceptual request through a clearly labeled simplified pipeline.

## Evaluation plan

For each niche, ship at least 6 comparable creative variants with a stable topic difficulty and length band. Randomize publishing sequence where feasible; do not treat individual viral outliers as proof. Log platform, time, topic, length, hook text, hook visual, style ID, views, engaged views when available, average view duration, average percentage viewed, retention at 1/3/5 seconds, completion rate, shares, saves, comments, and follow rate. Document which metrics differ by platform. Change **one primary creative variable** per A/B pair (hook vs hook, not hook+topic+duration together). Optimize for retention and satisfaction without clickbait or unsupported claims.

## Risk controls

- Do not declare a style "viral" based on design alone.
- Source each statistic, quote, product performance claim and named historical event before publication.
- Do not copy other creators' graphics or reuse third-party assets without appropriate license.
- Affiliate videos should disclose relevant commercial relationships under applicable requirements.
- Use the visual grammar of each reference style, but create original layouts, assets and scripts.
